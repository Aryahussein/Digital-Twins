* NOISE TEST 5: NMOS with small KF to see thermal/flicker crossover
* KF=1e-31 gives corner frequency around 1kHz (visible in sweep)
* Below ~1kHz: flicker dominates  (rising 1/f slope)
* Above ~1kHz: thermal dominates  (flat)
VDD 3 0 DC 5
VGS 1 0 DC 2.0
RD  3 2 10000
M1  2 1 0 0 NMOS
.MODEL NMOS VTO=0.7 KP=100u KF=1e-31
.NOISE V(2) VDD DEC 30 1 1MEG
.END

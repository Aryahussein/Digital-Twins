* NOISE TEST 2: Two resistors in series — noise adds
* Both R1 and R2 contribute thermal noise independently
* Total at low freq: S_v ~ 4kT*(R1+R2) = 4kT*3000 = 4.94e-17 V^2/Hz
* R2 contributes more because it sees all its noise go to output
* R1 contributes less at high freq (C1 shorts it out)
V1 1 0 AC 1
R1 1 2 1000
R2 2 3 2000
C1 3 0 1e-6
.NOISE V(3) V1 DEC 30 1 10000
.END

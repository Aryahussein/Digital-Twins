* --- Optimized DRAM Netlist ---
* Transistor: Sized for high current to charge the smaller cap quickly
M1 BL WL SN 0 MMOD L=0.18u W=2u

* Storage Capacitor: 100fF is typical for high-density DRAM
* This allows the voltage to swing quickly during your pulses.
C_storage SN 0 100fF

* Leakage Resistor: Adjusted to see visible decay over 25us
* 100Meg gives a time constant (RC) of 10ms. 
* 10Meg gives a time constant of 1ms. 
R_leak SN 0 10Meg

* --- NMOS Model ---
.MODEL MMOD NMOS (VTO=0.7 KP=200u)

* --- Signals for Full Life-Cycle (Write 1 -> Hold -> Write 0) ---
* BL: High for 10us (to write 1), then Low for 10us (to write 0)
V_BL BL 0 PULSE(0 5 0 80n 0n 10u 20u)

* WL: Pulse twice. 
* Once at 1us to store the '1'. 
* Once at 12us to clear it ('Write 0').
V_WL WL 0 PWL(0 0 1u 0 1.3u 5 3u 5 3.3u 0 12u 0 12.3u 5 14u 5 14.3u 0)

* --- Simulation ---
.TRAN 10n 10u

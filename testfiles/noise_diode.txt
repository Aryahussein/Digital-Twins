* NOISE TEST 3: Diode shot noise
* Forward bias: Id ~ 0.28 mA at V=0.65V
* Shot noise current: S_id = 2*q*Id = 2*1.6e-19*0.28e-3 = 9.0e-23 A^2/Hz
* This current develops across the parallel R1||rd at output
* R1 also contributes thermal noise
V1 1 0 DC 0.65
R1 1 2 100
D1 2 0 1e-14
.AC DEC 20 1 1MEG
.NOISE V(2) V1 DEC 20 1 1MEG
.END
